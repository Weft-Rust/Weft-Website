  • Blocks: The block builder and everything a block's rules, hazards and states are made with. (94 methods)
  • Items: The item builder. (30 methods)
  • Entities: Entity kinds, their AI, riding, status effects, poses, game modes and damage causes. (184 methods)
  • World generation: Dimensions, biomes, terrain, features, structures and the sky. (217 methods)
  • World: What a World can do, shapes, directions, conditions. (195 methods)
  • Registration: The registry, components, tags, settings, slots and menus. (218 methods)
  • Client: The client side: input, camera, screen text, packets. (106 methods)
  • UI: Screens, HUD parts and the styles they use. (106 methods)
  • Recipes and loot: Recipe kinds, recipes, loot tables and their pools. (28 methods)
