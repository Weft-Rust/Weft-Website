Camera modes

A camera mode is a value like any other: where the camera is, what goes with it and which keys switch to it.

camera::BOB enables walking bob, hurt tilt and held-item recoil without FOV changes or screen overlays. The third-person views and shot::behind use it; EFFECTS includes the same motion along with the first-person effects.

r.camera(&ORBIT).shot(shot::orbit(6.0)).key(&ORBIT_KEY, "O").transition(0.5, easing::SMOOTH) is a camera of your own. A shot runs every frame on the client and says where the camera is and looks, from the keys, the player's eyes and last frame's camera; shot::eyes, behind, orbit, free, top_down, fixed and path (a cutscene through points) are ready, Shot::new(&[camera::BODY, camera::HUD], |a| a.out = ..) is yours, with camera being abi::comp::camera. Its tags say what goes with it: BODY, HAND, HUD, EFFECTS (bob, sprint FOV, hurt tilt), CLIP (pulled in where a block is in the way, as third person), SOLID (a small box that bumps into blocks and stays where the world is loaded), DETACHED (keys and mouse move the camera, the player stands), AIM, NO_INTERACT, CYCLE (F5 goes through it), ASK (only where the server lets it). The engine's first person, behind, front and free camera (P) are modes too: r.edit(&cameras::FRONT).untag(camera::CYCLE) takes the front view out of F5. The free camera asks question::CAMERA first, allowed in a game mode that flies; Verdict::Allow lets it anywhere, Deny nowhere (a.is_camera(&cameras::FREE)). The server puts a player in one with w.force_camera(player, &MODE, Some((2.0, easing::IN_OUT))), held until w.release_camera(..), or w.set_camera(..) it may leave. ease(easing::BOUNCE_OUT, t) is the same curves for your own moves. The world still loads around the player, not the camera.
